# Luminous depth pass

Responds to feedback that the antique gold and purple surfaces had become too muted. Gold now uses #C99B58, #DFB56D highlights and #B38748 metal shading. Amethyst cards have brighter directional illumination while the page frame remains obsidian. No white stops in gold gradients.

The shared surface system adds distinct purple surface planes, inset illuminated edges, contact shadows and broader cast shadows. Command receives stronger moving light fields and a brighter Cassius core. Existing pointer-driven reflections and bounded card tilt extend to record, glass and learning panels. Editing fields and focused cards are excluded from tilt by the existing controller. Motion still respects pause, reduced motion, page visibility and intersection state. No new animation loops or dependencies.

No data contracts or functionality changes. Contrast-sensitive and forced-color fallbacks remain. Automated checks verify code and routes; rendered visual quality and device performance are not claimed as measured.
