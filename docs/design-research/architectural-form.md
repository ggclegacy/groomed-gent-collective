# Architectural form: permanent palette, stronger construction

## Research completed before implementation

Porsche Design describes functional, restrained design: https://www.porsche.com/stories/design/7-porsche-design-products-that-stunned-the-world/

Audemars Piguet documents the Royal Oak's bevelled geometry, machining and contrasts between satin and polished finishes: https://apchronicles.audemarspiguet.com/en/article/the-first-royal-oak-cases

Bentley describes material craftsmanship and deliberate detailing: https://www.bentleymotors.com/en/about-bentley/people-and-expertise/craftsmanship.html

These are references for precision and substantial luxury, not evidence that a shape has an inherent gender. For this member audience and requested masculine direction, use architectural geometry, restrained detailing, strong upright type and controlled movement.

## Built

Tighter 4–6px panel/control corners replace inflated rounding throughout shared, Voyage, Product Studio, Product Mastery, Creator Studio and personal-workspace styles. Preserve circular data charts, avatars and Cassius's optical sphere where circles communicate their purpose. Upright sans-serif display typography replaces italic editorial headings. Existing colors and gradients remain unchanged.

New decorative SVG architecture replaces floating beads, soft contours and the tilted miniature monolith in Command's cards. A static octagonal chassis gives the animated Cassius sphere a mechanical frame. Rectilinear background paths replace elliptical decorative sweeps; decorative stars are removed. Existing motion pause, offscreen, reduced-motion and hidden-tab handling remains. No new dependencies, request loops or fake data.

The form layer is imported last. Decorative geometry is aria-hidden and noninteractive; button hit areas and keyboard focus remain. Forced-color rules remove nonessential graphics and preserve native contrast. Work is isolated from the unrelated active intelligence backend changes.
