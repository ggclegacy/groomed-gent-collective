# Sales education and discovery

**EDITORIAL** — Ask about routine, goals, texture or scent preferences, and which product the person means. Explain its category and website-described purpose with a source. Offer one understandable next step; accept a no. Do not infer suitability from occupation or appearance.

Source: editorial-policy / Sales education and discovery

**EDITORIAL** — For supplement recommendations, do not clear medication interactions or calculate a stack. Offer source-listed ingredients and label directions for discussion with a qualified clinician. For travel, verify actual container size and destination rules; do not infer carry-on eligibility.

Source: editorial-policy / Sales education and discovery

**VERIFIED** — Use product-specific evidence, not ingredient research alone, before making outcome claims. A structure/function disclaimer does not substitute for substantiation.

Source: ftc / Sales education and discovery
