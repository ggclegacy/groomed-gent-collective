# Research ingestion roadmap

The infrastructure is installed; expert knowledge must now be acquired and reviewed. No scientific grooming claim was released by this implementation. Topic count is a coverage measure, never a claim of mastery.

## Ordered research program

| Phase | Scope | Release gate |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Evidence rules, consultation scope and evaluation cases (D18–D19) | Review owners assigned; scope and abstention cases accepted |
| P1 | Hair/scalp foundations, barbering, beard, shaving, skin, body and tools (D01–D06, D14, D16) | Foundational dossiers and practice cases approved |
| P2 | Ingredients, chemistry, formats, soap and scent (D07–D12) | Material identity, formulation limits and source applicability reviewed |
| P3 | Development, quality, claims and GGC product matching (D17–D19) | Manufacturer evidence and separate Product Studio/claim approvals |
| P4 | Heritage (D15), alongside later phases | Primary provenance, rights and cultural attribution reviewed |
| P5 | Frontier (D13) after foundation/product-science phases | Human/preclinical distinction and surveillance ownership established |

Modules carry explicit prerequisites; phase grouping never overrides the module graph. For example, cosmetic chemistry foundations precede ingredient-dossier depth, and regulation/evidence foundations precede frontier appraisal. Start with the four batches in `ingestion-roadmap.json`, then progress through Foundation → Mastery → Advanced → Frontier per domain. Do not delay core practical coverage to chase an emerging claim.

## Source discovery and selection

The source seeds are discovery pointers checked through web search on September 5, 2026. They do not represent full-text review, scientific endorsement or a licensed copy of a textbook. The initial scope was cross-checked against professional barbering and cosmetic-chemistry education and regulator/clinical reference entry points:

- [Milady barbering curriculum](https://www.milady.com/catalog/milady-standard-barbering-6th-edition) and [SCC Beginning Cosmetic Chemistry](https://www.scconline.org/Education/Certificate-Program/2024-Skin-Care-Required/Beginning-Cosmetic-Chemistry): find the appropriate editions and licensed teaching materials.
- [AAD Hair Loss Resource Center](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/hair-loss) and [AAD razor-bump guidance](https://www.aad.org/news/how-to-prevent-razor-bumps): locate applicable clinical guidance and its underlying evidence.
- [Cosmetic Ingredient Review](https://www.cir-safety.org/) and [IFRA Standards Library](https://ifrafragrance.org/standards-library): acquire material- and use-category-specific assessments with version dates.
- [FDA MoCRA overview](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/modernization-cosmetics-regulation-act-2022-mocra) and [FDA Cosmetics Labeling Claims](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-labeling/cosmetics-labeling-claims): begin jurisdiction-specific regulatory research; check current authoritative text before application.

Expand beyond these seeds: peer-reviewed primary studies and registered trials; systematic reviews with included-study extraction; licensed professional technique curricula; standards publishers and local licensing boards; manufacturer specifications for exact materials/models; primary archives and community-authored oral histories. D15 currently has no discovered source seed. EU/UK requirements, FTC guidance, occupational safety and device documentation also need specific acquisition. Search snippets, commercial summaries and the originating chat are not evidence substitutes.

## Per-topic ingestion procedure

1. Select a stable topic ID and review its prerequisites, research questions and expected deliverables.
2. Create source records with edition/version, publication/access dates, publisher, jurisdiction, rights, visibility, correction/retraction check and reviewer. Preserve permitted source bytes and SHA-256; use link-only records where reproduction is restricted.
3. Extract atomic claims with exact source locator and short supporting excerpt. Record study design, sample, comparator, duration, outcomes, effect estimate, uncertainty, funding and applicable population/material/exposure. Capture opposing findings separately.
4. Populate ingredient/technique/device/development/heritage dossiers using claim references. Do not replace unknown concentration, formula or safety data with guesses.
5. Appraise relevance, methods, consistency and uncertainty. Use the evidence categories in `evidence-policy.json`; retain medical/professional scope separately. Record unresolved conflicts.
6. Obtain domain-specialist and evidence review; add regulatory review where claims, devices or jurisdictional rules require it. Reviewers must be real identified people, not fictitious attestations or autogenerated approvals.
7. Validate schemas and references, check source integrity and rights, and run retrieval/consultation regression cases. A public summary must omit confidential data and preserve relevant limits. Approve commercial wording separately for the exact product, formula and jurisdiction.
8. Move approved claims into the release registry; implement applicability-aware retrieval before enabling answers. Record release version and changelog, retain the prior release for rollback, and invalidate affected caches on withdrawal.

## Maintenance and measurement

Default review intervals: foundational/history records 365 days; practical/product science 180 days; clinical/commercial records 90 days; frontier 30 days. These are editorial scheduling defaults, not validity guarantees. Safety alerts, retractions, new contradictory evidence, formula changes and standards amendments trigger immediate reassessment. Expired or withdrawn evidence is withheld rather than silently reused. No automatic monitoring or scheduler was created.

Track planned/acquired/appraised/reviewed topics, released claims, expired reviews, unresolved conflicts, citation precision, product identity mismatches and consultation escalation/abstention quality. Mastery completion requires the module's case assessment and qualified review; a downloaded document alone does not complete a module.
