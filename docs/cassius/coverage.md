## Highest-priority gaps

- Signed current manufacturer formula/INCI and label artwork for every SKU/flavor, plus effective dates, batch/COA and allergen/cross-contact information. Six Reserve products have catalog ingredient declarations despite missing visible INCI sections; those declarations are not certified complete.
- Barber’s Blend: reconcile 11 versus 12 ingredients, broccoli oil, amber versus vanilla scent descriptions, full fragrance/INCI, current size and founder-rate pricing.
- Hydros: Lemonade appears in a page menu but lacks a catalog listing or verified formula. Reconcile B6 form, omitted B5 and colorant language per flavor. Never use another flavor’s label as proof.
- Fortius: resolve $52.99 versus $49.99. Ascend: reconcile 150 g net weight versus 20 × 8 g servings and maximum intake. Genesis: resolve 1–2 servings versus two capsules and retain CFU-at-manufacture qualification.
- Nocturne: proprietary blend’s individual amounts remain undisclosed; no guessing. Confirm latest safety/interaction label.
- Claims: product-specific substantiation, approved language/channel/market and legal review. Ingredient studies, marketing claims, clinical efficacy and brand approval remain separate.
- Commercial policies: approved ambassador commission/discount/payout/attribution terms, wholesale launch/pricing/minimums, current refund/terms documents. Retail listing does not establish wholesale availability.
- Provider directory: independently validated credentials, current locations, relationships and offers. No human-use recommendation for research compounds.
- Training: manufacturer-backed modules, reviewed curriculum, assessment rubrics and certification authority. Existing Product Studio concepts remain drafts.
- Founder: owner-reviewed biography/date chronology, beyond the published brand story.
