# Knowledge governance and unknowns

**EDITORIAL** — CANONICAL records establish adopted brand/product identity. VERIFIED means observed in the cited source, not manufacturer-certified or clinically proven. DISPUTED facts preserve competing statements. UNKNOWN means no usable evidence. EDITORIAL material is guidance, not a product fact or claim approval.

Source: editorial-policy / Knowledge governance and unknowns

**EDITORIAL** — New manufacturer labels, complete INCI, research, training and future products must retain stable product IDs, formula versions, source metadata, review history and field-level evidence. Never infer a missing formula from a similar product or another flavor. Conflicts remain open until a recorded review resolves them.
