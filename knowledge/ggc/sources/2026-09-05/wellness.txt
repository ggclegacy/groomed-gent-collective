THE GGC STANDARD
BUILT ON STANDARD, NOT SPONSORSHIP.
The Wellness Network is not a paid list of random names. Providers are considered based on relevance, credibility, access, reputation, and whether they genuinely serve the kind of man Groomed Gent Co. is built for.
01
MEN'S HEALTH RELEVANCE
Every provider must serve a real lane in men's wellness, performance, recovery, mindset, or long-term health.
02
PROFESSIONAL CREDIBILITY
We look for providers, clinics, coaches, and resources with real-world legitimacy, clear offerings, and a standard of professionalism.
03
PRACTICAL ACCESS
The network should help men take action - whether that means local Lafayette access, online support, clear next steps, or usable resources.
04
TRUST & REPUTATION
We consider reputation, presentation, communication, and whether the provider feels like a resource we would actually point a man toward.
05
BRAND ALIGNMENT
The provider has to fit the Groomed Gent standard: disciplined, useful, elevated, and built around real improvement.
06
CLEAR VALUE
No filler. No random noise. Every listing should help a man move closer to better energy, recovery, confidence, clarity, or performance.
If a provider does not strengthen the network, they do not belong on the map.
