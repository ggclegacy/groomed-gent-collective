OUR STORY
BUILT BY SOMEONE WHO NEEDED IT FIRST.
Groomed Gent Co. wasn't built from a business plan. It was built from a life — and everything that life taught one man about what it means to take care of yourself.
◆
THE ARCHITECT
NEIL STUTES
Neil Stutes spent most of his life showing up for everyone else before he learned to show up for himself. Low self-confidence followed him through the oil fields, through restaurant management, through 15,000 pharmaceutical deliveries across Lafayette. He showed up every day — worked hard, took care of people — but struggled with how he saw himself.
Then he found the barbershop. Not just as a place to get a cut — but as a culture. A space where a man sits down with the full weight of his week and stands up feeling reset. Sharp. Present. Like himself again. Neil felt that transformation personally. He watched it happen to the men around him. And he couldn't stop thinking about why that feeling never lasted beyond the door.
So he walked away from the safety of traditional work and built something around that gap. Not because it was the easy path — because it was the only one that made sense. Groomed Gent Co. is the brand he needed when he was still figuring himself out. He's building it so other men don't have to figure it out alone.
"I'm not building this for me. I'm building it for every man who ever sat in that chair and felt something shift."
THE ROAD HERE
NOTHING ABOUT THIS BRAND WAS BUILT IN A STRAIGHT LINE.
That's what makes it real.
THE OIL FIELD & THE GRIND
Years of hard physical work in the Louisiana oil industry. Learning what it means to show up every day regardless of how you feel. Building toughness. Building discipline. Building the foundation of a work ethic that never left.
RUNNING THE RESTAURANT
Managing high-volume restaurant operations. Learning people. Learning pressure. Learning how to lead when everything is moving fast and nothing is going right. Skills that never show up on a résumé but show up in everything.
THE APOTHECARY SHOPPE
3.5 years. 15,000+ deliveries across Lafayette. Patient-facing pharmaceutical work that put Neil in front of real people managing real health challenges — and showed him firsthand how deeply wellness and daily life are connected.
THE FILM SET
Background work on Free State of Jones with Matthew McConaughey. Present on set — but not fully present in himself yet. A reminder that life doesn't wait for you to be ready. And that sometimes the most important thing you can build is the man who shows up.
THE BARBERSHOP
This is where it changed. Not one moment — a pattern. A man sits down carrying everything. He stands up different. Neil felt it. He watched it happen to others. He decided to build something that kept that feeling alive.
GROOMED GENT CO.
Founded in Lafayette, Louisiana. Born in barbershop culture. Built for the whole man. Still being built — one disciplined day at a time.
THE MISSION
THIS ISN'T ABOUT GROOMING.
It never was. Grooming is the entry point — the moment a man decides he's worth taking care of. But Groomed Gent Co. was built for everything that comes after that moment. The health he starts paying attention to. The confidence that starts showing up at work, at home, in how he carries himself. The legacy he starts thinking about building.
Neil built this brand because he spent too many years not believing he was worth the investment. He knows what it costs a man to walk through life that way — and he knows what changes when he stops. GCC exists for every man who's ready to stop.
"Every man has a legacy in him. This brand exists to help him build it."
